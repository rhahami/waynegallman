About

Wayne Gallman II

Wayne Gallman II grew up in Loganville, Georgia and played at Grayson High School before signing with Clemson, where he redshirted a year and then rewrote his role in the offense. He ran for 1,514 yards and 13 touchdowns as a redshirt sophomore, added 1,133 yards and 17 touchdowns the following season, and was named second-team All-ACC twice.

In January 2017 he finished the job Clemson had been chasing: a College Football Playoff National Championship, won 35–31 over Alabama. Months later the New York Giants took him in the fourth round of the NFL Draft.

He spent four seasons in New York, and his 2020 campaign was his most productive as a pro — 682 rushing yards and 6 touchdowns on 147 carries. From there came the part of football nobody puts on a poster: stops with San Francisco, Atlanta and Minnesota, practice-squad time in Kansas City and Seattle, and a return to the field with the St. Louis BattleHawks.

Wayne knows what it looks like to be the guy everyone's watching, and what it looks like to be the guy fighting to be seen. Today he partners with brands and speaks to rooms that want something more useful than a highlight reel.
